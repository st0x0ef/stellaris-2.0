Cables carry energy between your machines.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/cable width=96 height=96]

Connect generators such as the [ref=stellaris:machines/solar_panel]Solar Panel[ref], the [ref=stellaris:machines/coal_generator]Coal Generator[ref] or the [ref=stellaris:machines/diesel_generator]Diesel Generator[ref] to the machines that need power, and store the surplus in a [ref=stellaris:machines/power_bank]Power Bank[ref]. 

Cables carry energy, never fluids. For fluids use [ref=stellaris:machines/pipes]Pipes[ref] instead. 



**Stats :** 
- Transfer Rate : 20 FE/t
