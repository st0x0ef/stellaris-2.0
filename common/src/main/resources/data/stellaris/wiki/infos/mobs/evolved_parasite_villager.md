This is an Evolved Parasite Villager. It is the evolved form of the [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite_villager]Parasite Villager[ref].

[entity=stellaris:parasite_affected_villager_evolved scale=25 width=150 rotation=(0,180,0)]

Unlike its earlier form, this one hunts. It actively chases players, villagers and iron golems. 



**Infection aura** 

Every 1.5 seconds it infects every living creature within 4 blocks, whether it has hit them or not. Staying next to it is enough to get sick. 



**Attacks** 

It has three attack variants, and all of them apply Weakness and the infection : 
- Claw : a straight melee hit. 
- Tentacle : adds Slowness for 3 seconds. 
- Spit : splashes everyone within about 2 blocks of the target. 

Only a [ref=stellaris:items/vaccine]Vaccine[ref] makes you immune to all of it. 



Stats : 
- Health : 44.0 
- Speed : 0.31 
- Damage : 7.0 
- Armor : 6.0 
- Knockback Resistance : 0.55
