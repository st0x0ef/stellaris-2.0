The launch pad towers are very cool... Yea they are just towers, but they are very cool!!

[item=stellaris:launch_pad_towers]

Right-click the centre of a [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket_launch_pad]Rocket Launch Pad[ref] with them in hand and the service arms rise around the pad. They change nothing about how the rocket flies, they simply make the launch look like a real one. 

Break the pad and the towers pop back out as an item, so you never lose them.
