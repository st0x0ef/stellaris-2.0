Silicon is the best semiconductor in the universe.

[item=stellaris:silicon]

Smelt or blast Nether Quartz to get it. 

The Stellar Company uses it in all of its electronic devices : the [ref=stellaris:items/sd_card]SD Card[ref], the [ref=stellaris:items/oil_finder]Oil Finder[ref], the [ref=stellaris:machines/antenna]Antenna[ref] and the [ref=stellaris:machines/engineering_station]Engineering Station[ref] all need it.
