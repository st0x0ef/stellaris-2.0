A bare [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket]Rocket[ref] flies, but it does not do much else. Modules are what turn it into a real ship.

[item=stellaris:cargo_module]

Install them in the Upgrade tab of the [ref=stellaris:machines/engineering_station]Engineering Station[ref] : rocket on the left, module in the middle, upgraded rocket on the right. The modules stay on the rocket item, so they survive landings and rebuilds. 



**Cargo Module** 

Raises the rocket's hold from 1 row to 3 rows. Everything in the hold comes back down with you inside the [ref=stellaris:rocket/lander]Lander[ref]. 



**Hydrogen Motor** 

Switches the engine from Fuel over to Hydrogen. Empty the tank before installing it, a tank only holds one kind of fuel at a time. 

[item=stellaris:hydrogen_motor]



**Autopilot Module** 

Right-click the module in hand to choose a destination, then install it. With an autopilot on board the rocket flies straight to that world by itself instead of opening the destination screen, and the lander comes down gently instead of exploding on impact. 

[item=stellaris:autopilot_module]



**Shield Module** 

Adds heat shields to the hull. 

[item=stellaris:shield_module]



**Skins** 

Purely cosmetic repaints of the hull : Galaxy, Frost and Military. 



**Rocket Models** 

Change the shape and size of the ship itself : Tiny, Small and Big rocket models.
