This is a Parasite Villager. Scientists have discovered that the [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite]parasite[ref] transforms regular villagers into this creature.

[entity=stellaris:parasite_affected_villager scale=25 width=150 rotation=(0,180,0)]

It never hunts on its own : it only strikes back at whoever hurts it first. 
Every hit it lands infects you for 16 seconds and slows you down for 4 seconds. 

Kill one and there is a 35% chance a [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite]Lunar Parasite[ref] crawls out of the corpse. 



**Evolution** 

Beware! After about two minutes of life, a wounded Parasite Villager (below 70% health) can evolve at any moment into an [ref=stellaris:mobs/evolved_parasite_villager]Evolved Parasite Villager[ref]. 
Finishing them off quickly is safer than leaving them injured. 



They do not spawn randomly on the Moon : you will meet them inside Moon villages and other lunar structures. 



Stats : 
- Health : 26.0 
- Speed : 0.28 
- Damage : 4.0 
- Armor : 2.0 
- Knockback Resistance : 0.2
