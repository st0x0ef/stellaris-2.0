The Gravity Manipulator changes the gravity of the chunk it stands in. It is what makes a space station or a low gravity world feel like home.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/gravity_manipulator width=96 height=96]



**How to use :** 
Place the Gravity Manipulator in the chunk you want to fix, connect it to a power source, then open the GUI and pick the gravity you want. Everything standing in that chunk falls at the new value : jump height, fall speed and fall damage all follow. 

It only works while it has energy. Cut the power and the chunk goes back to the natural gravity of the planet, which is 9.81 on Earth, 1.62 on the Moon and 0.5 in orbit. 

If several manipulators sit in the same chunk, their corrections are averaged. 

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/gui/gravity_manipulator_gui width=145 height=96]



**Stats :** 
- Power Consumption : 1 FE/t 
- Gravity Range : 0 to 20 
- Area : the chunk the machine is in 
- Internal Buffer : 3 000 FE
