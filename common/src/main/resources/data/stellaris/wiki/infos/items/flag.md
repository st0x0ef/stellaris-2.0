The flag is a decoration block that you can customize. Plant one where you land and the place is yours.

[item=stellaris:flag]

The flag is three blocks tall, so leave room above it when you place it. It automatically shows the head of whoever planted it. 

Right-click it with a player head to put that player's face on it instead. 
Right-click it with a dye to change the color of the cloth. 

Both the color and the head are remembered by the item, so a flag you break and place again comes back exactly as you left it.
