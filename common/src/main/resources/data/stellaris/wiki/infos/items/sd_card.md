The SD card is a small memory chip left behind by earlier missions. It contains some very valuable information...

[item=stellaris:sd_card]

Search the chests of the structures scattered across the worlds and you will find them : satellites and operation bases on Earth, meteors, and on the Moon the villages, spheres, dungeons and abandoned space stations. Every card carries a different log, and read in order they tell what really happened up there. 

Research at the [ref=stellaris:machines/laboratory]Laboratory[ref] also rewards you with one card per stage. 



How do you read it? 
The Stellar Company has developed a special application to read them. Open your [ref=stellaris:items/tablet]Tablet[ref] and launch the SD Card reader. 

You can also craft a blank one from [ref=stellaris:items/silicon]Silicon[ref], gold nuggets and redstone, though a blank card has nothing to tell.
