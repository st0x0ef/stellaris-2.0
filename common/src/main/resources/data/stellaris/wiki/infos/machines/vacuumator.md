The Vacuumator is a machine that is used to put food into [ref=stellaris:items/cans]Cans[ref].

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/vacuumator width=96 height=96]



How to use: 
Place the vacuumator. From left to right, place a [ref=stellaris:items/cans]Can[ref], your food, and an empty bottle. Then you will obtain a can of food and a bottle of water.
