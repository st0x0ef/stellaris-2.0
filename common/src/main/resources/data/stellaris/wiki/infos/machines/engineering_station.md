The Engineering Station is a very important machine.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/engineering_station width=96 height=96]

[item=stellaris:engineering_station onlyIcon]

This station is used to build [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket]rockets[ref]. 
You can also use it to upgrade your rockets and help you explore more planets! 



**Space Station Planner** : 
Use the rocket planner to build space station plans. Select your space station, place a space station blueprint and then add the needed resources to build the station.
