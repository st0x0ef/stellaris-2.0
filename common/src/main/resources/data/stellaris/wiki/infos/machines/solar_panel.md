The Solar Panel is a basic power generator that runs on sunlight. It never needs refueling, which makes it the most convenient generator for a permanent base.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/solar_panel width=96 height=96]



How to use : 
Place the solar panel where the block directly above it can see the sky, and connect it to your power grid. It produces energy during the day and stops at night. Pair it with a [ref=stellaris:machines/star_light_panel]Star Light Panel[ref] to cover the night. 

It also has an item slot : put a chargeable item in it, such as an [ref=stellaris:items/oil_finder]Oil Finder[ref] or a [ref=stellaris:items/space_suit]Space Suit[ref] helmet, and the panel will refill it. 



Stats : 
- Power Output : 1 FE/t 
- Item Charging Rate : 10 FE/t 
- Internal Buffer : 12 800 FE
