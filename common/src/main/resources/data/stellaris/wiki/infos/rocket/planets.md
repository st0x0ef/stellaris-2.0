Every world you can reach has its own gravity, its own atmosphere and its own weather. Check them on the Planets application of your [ref=stellaris:items/tablet]Tablet[ref] before you take off.

[item=stellaris:rocket_nose_cone]

**Earth** 

Gravity 9.81. It has oxygen, so you can breathe, eat, farm and light fires freely. 
[ref=stellaris:items/titanium]Titanium[ref] and [ref=stellaris:items/oil]Oil[ref] are both found here. 



**Earth Orbit** 

Gravity 0.5. No oxygen. Temperature -270 degrees, day and night. 
This is the only place where you may build a space station. 



**The Moon** 

Gravity 1.62. No oxygen. Temperature +123 degrees during the day and -233 at night. 
It holds [ref=stellaris:items/titanium]Titanium[ref], the only [ref=stellaris:items/desh]Desh[ref] in the game, iron in its stone, [ref=stellaris:items/blue_liquid]Blue Liquid[ref] lakes, and the villages, spheres and dungeons where the [ref=stellaris:items/sd_card]SD Cards[ref] are hidden. 
It is also where the [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite]Lunar Parasite[ref] lives. 



**Surviving off Earth** 

Any world without oxygen deals 0.5 damage per second to whatever breathes. There are two answers : wear a full [ref=stellaris:items/space_suit]Space Suit[ref] with air in the helmet, or seal a room and fill it with an [ref=stellaris:machines/oxygen]Oxygen Distributor[ref]. 
Low gravity changes how far you jump and how hard you fall. A [ref=stellaris:machines/gravity_manipulator]Gravity Manipulator[ref] brings a chunk back to whatever gravity you prefer.
