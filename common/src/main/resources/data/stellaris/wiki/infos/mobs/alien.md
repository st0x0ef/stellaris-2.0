The Alien is an intelligent and peaceful creature encountered during space exploration.

[entity=stellaris:alien scale=30 width=150 height=100]

Aliens take on a biome-appropriate type and one of thirteen professions, so they are always ready to trade with you - even without a job-site block nearby. 
Their trades are their own : each profession has its own alien trade tables, so do not expect the deals you know from Earth. 

They are passive and will not attack you, they breed like villagers, and they never call an iron golem for help. 
They also do not need oxygen and cannot catch the infection. 



Stats : 
- Health : 20.0 
- Speed : 0.5 
- Follow Range : 48.0
