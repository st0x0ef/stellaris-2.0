The Star Crawler is an armored hostile creature, a leftover of an experiment that got out of hand.

[entity=stellaris:star_crawler scale=35 width=150]

Its natural armor makes it tougher than most monsters, and it will relentlessly pursue players and villagers. 
It does not spawn on its own anywhere yet : for now you only meet one if it is released with a spawn egg. 



**Attacks** 

- Leap : it winds up, then throws itself at you for 6.5 damage and poisons you for 4 seconds. While airborne it deflects most hits, so do not waste your swing. 
- Whiplash : it sweeps its body around itself, hitting everything from 0.5 to 4.5 blocks away for 4.5 damage and throwing them back. 
- Spin : once it rolls fast enough, simply touching it hurts. 

After a big attack it needs a moment to recover. That window is when you strike. 



**Drops** 

- Lunar Crawler Spike Cluster 



Stats : 
- Health : 26.0 
- Speed : 0.27 
- Damage : 5.0 
- Armor : 3.0 
- Knockback Resistance : 0.15
