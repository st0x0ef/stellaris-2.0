The Lander is what actually brings you down. You never craft one : it appears around you the moment your rocket arrives above a new world.

[item=stellaris:rocket]

You start the descent sitting inside it, together with your rocket, its fuel and everything that was in its hold. Ride it down and you land with all your gear. 

**It explodes on impact.** The blast is strong enough to dig a crater and to hurt you badly, so land away from anything you care about. A rocket fitted with an [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket_modules]Autopilot Module[ref] sets its lander down gently instead. 

After the landing the lander stays on the ground as a container holding the rocket and your cargo. Empty it by hand, or let a [ref=stellaris:machines/cargo_unloader]Cargo Unloader[ref] placed nearby pull everything out for you. 



Stats : 
- Inventory : 30 slots 
- Explosion Power : 10, unless an autopilot is installed
