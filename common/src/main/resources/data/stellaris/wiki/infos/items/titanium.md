Titanium is a very useful resource for space travel.

[item=stellaris:titanium_ingot]

Titanium is used in so many places. Its hardness allows the [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket]rocket[ref] to survive extreme temperatures encountered during space travel.

Titanium is also used in nearly all of the machines manufactured by The Stellar Company.

[item=stellaris:titanium_ore]

Titanium ore can be found on both Earth and Moon under y = 32.
