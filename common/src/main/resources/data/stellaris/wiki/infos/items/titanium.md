Titanium is the backbone of space travel.

[item=stellaris:titanium_ingot]

Its hardness allows the [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket]rocket[ref] to survive the extreme temperatures of space travel, and nearly every machine manufactured by The Stellar Company needs some.

[item=stellaris:titanium_ore]

Titanium ore is found on **both Earth and the Moon**, below y = 32. On Earth it also appears in its deepslate form. Mine it for Raw Titanium, then smelt or blast it into an ingot. 
Nuggets, blocks, plating blocks, slabs, stairs and pillars all exist, and eight Titanium Plating Blocks in a ring make a [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket_launch_pad]Rocket Launch Pad[ref]. 



**Titanium tools** 

A full tool set can be forged from it, sitting between iron and diamond. 
- Durability : 1 200 
- Mining Speed : 6.0 
- Attack Bonus : 2.0 
- Enchantability : 14
