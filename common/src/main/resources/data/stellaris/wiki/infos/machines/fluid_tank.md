The Fluid Tank is used to store any kind of fluid.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/fluid_tank width=96 height=96]



**How to use:** 
Place the Fluid Tank. Now you can connect [ref=stellaris:machines/pipes]Pipes[ref] to it to input and output fluids, or fill and empty it with a bucket or a [ref=stellaris:items/fluid_cell]Fluid Cell[ref]. 
One tank holds one kind of fluid at a time. 



**Stats :** 
- Tier 1 Capacity : 5 000 mB
