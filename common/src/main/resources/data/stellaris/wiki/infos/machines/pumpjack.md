The Pumpjack is the machine that allows you to extract oil from the ground. It is an essential machine for space exploration, as oil is required to create diesel fuel, which powers the rocket.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/pumpjack width=96 height=96]

[item=stellaris:pumpjack onlyIcon]



How to use: 
Place the Pumpjack in the desired chunk. Connect the machine to a power source, then open the GUI to see the amount of oil in the chunk. 



Warning: 
Place the pumpjack carefully! When you break the pumpjack, you will get almost all the items needed to craft it except the pumpjack drill.
