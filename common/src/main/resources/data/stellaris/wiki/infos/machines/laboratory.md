The Laboratory is where you study the parasite infection and develop a cure.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/laboratory width=96 height=96]

It offers two functions : 
- Research a pathogen sample captured from a [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite]Lunar Parasite[ref]. 
- Produce a [ref=stellaris:items/vaccine]Vaccine[ref] once your research is complete. 

The vaccine cures the infection and protects you from re-infection.
