The Laboratory is where you study the parasite infection and develop a cure.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/laboratory width=96 height=96]

It has two tabs : a Research tab and a Vaccine tab. 



**Research** 

Kill [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite]Lunar Parasites[ref] yourself to collect Parasites, then right-click with a Pathogen Storage Cell in hand to load every Parasite in your inventory into the cell. One cell holds up to 500. 

[item=stellaris:pathogen_storage_cell]

Put the filled cell in the Laboratory and press Research. The analysis takes 30 seconds and **empties the cell whether it succeeds or not**, so the more parasites you loaded, the better your odds. 

Research has six stages. Each successful stage gives you an SD card with a piece of the story, readable on your [ref=stellaris:items/tablet]Tablet[ref]. Roughly what a single run needs for a sure result : 
- Reaching stage 0 : 1 parasite 
- Reaching stage 1 : 100 parasites 
- Reaching stage 2 : 200 parasites 
- Reaching stage 3 : 300 parasites 
- Reaching stage 4 : 400 parasites 
- Reaching stage 5 : 500 parasites 



**Vaccine** 

Stage 4 names the last ingredient, so from there you can switch to the Vaccine tab and combine the four ingredients to craft a [ref=stellaris:items/vaccine]Vaccine[ref], which makes you permanently immune to the infection. 
Stage 5 only files the complete procedure : it adds nothing to the formula, and its SD card simply recaps the finished recipe.
