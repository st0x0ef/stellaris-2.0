When the colony started dying, the Stellar Company had nothing that could help. Its machines drill, refine and fly. None of them could tell anyone what was in the rock.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/laboratory width=96 height=96]

So they built a new one. The Laboratory is the Company's answer to the [ref=stellaris:mobs/infection]Infection[ref] : a machine whose only purpose is to take the parasite apart and work out what undoes it. Doctor Vey's team ran the first samples through one of these, and every conclusion they reached is still on the cards you find in the tunnels.

They finished the work. They simply finished it too late to save anyone. You are picking up a solved problem - you just have to solve it again, from the beginning, because the Company never got the data home.

The machine has two tabs : Research, and Vaccine.



**Research** 

You cannot study a parasite you did not kill. Cut down [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite]Lunar Parasites[ref] yourself to collect Parasites, then right-click with a Pathogen Storage Cell in hand to load every one you are carrying into the cell. A cell holds 500.

[item=stellaris:pathogen_storage_cell]

Put the filled cell into the Laboratory and press Research. The analysis runs for 30 seconds and **empties the cell whether it concludes anything or not**.

That is the whole difficulty of it. A thin sample is not a slower answer, it is a wasted one : the odds of the run succeeding are roughly the number of parasites you fed it, divided by how far along you already are. Half the sample is half the chance, and a failed run costs you every parasite in the cell.

There are five stages, and each one asks for more than the last. These are the amounts that make a run a certainty : 
- Stage 0 : 1 parasite - the first look is free 
- Stage 1 : 100 parasites 
- Stage 2 : 200 parasites 
- Stage 3 : 300 parasites 
- Stage 4 : 400 parasites 

Every stage you reach hands you an [ref=stellaris:items/sd_card]SD Card[ref] carrying that piece of the original research, readable on your [ref=stellaris:items/tablet]Tablet[ref]. Read them in order and you watch the Company's scientists work the problem out - first what the parasite is, then that honey holds it, then that honey alone is not enough, and finally the whole formula.



**Vaccine** 

Only once the last stage is done does the Vaccine tab become worth opening. Combine what the research told you : 
- A Honey Bottle 
- A [ref=stellaris:items/blue_liquid]Blue Liquid[ref] Bucket 
- An Astrum Vitreus Cluster 
- A Parasite 

The result is a [ref=stellaris:items/vaccine]Vaccine[ref] - the thing the colony died a few weeks short of. Drink it and the Moon can never hollow you out.
