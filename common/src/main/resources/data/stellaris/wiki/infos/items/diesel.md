Diesel and Fuel are the two refined products of the oil chain. One powers your base, the other powers your rocket.

[item=stellaris:diesel_bucket]

The chain starts with [ref=stellaris:items/oil]Oil[ref], which you locate with the [ref=stellaris:items/oil_finder]Oil Finder[ref] and extract using a [ref=stellaris:machines/pumpjack]Pumpjack[ref].

[item=stellaris:fuel_bucket]

The [ref=stellaris:machines/fuel_refinery]Fuel Refinery[ref] then splits oil into both at once : every 10 mB of oil becomes 5 mB of Fuel and 5 mB of Diesel. 

- **Diesel** feeds the [ref=stellaris:machines/diesel_generator]Diesel Generator[ref] and the [ref=stellaris:items/rover]Rover[ref]. 
- **Fuel** is what a [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket]Rocket[ref] burns to reach space. One launch costs 1 000 mB, and a rocket tank holds 3 000 mB. 

Both can be carried in buckets, in a [ref=stellaris:items/fluid_cell]Fluid Cell[ref], or in a tank module on your [ref=stellaris:items/space_suit]Space Suit[ref].
