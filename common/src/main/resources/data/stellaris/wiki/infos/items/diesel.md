Diesel and Fuel are the refined products of the oil chain, used to power machines and rockets.

[item=stellaris:diesel_bucket]

The chain starts with [ref=stellaris:items/oil]Oil[ref], which you locate with the [ref=stellaris:items/oil_finder]Oil Finder[ref] and extract using a [ref=stellaris:machines/pumpjack]Pumpjack[ref].

[item=stellaris:fuel_bucket]

The [ref=stellaris:machines/fuel_refinery]Fuel Refinery[ref] then turns oil into Fuel and Diesel. 
Diesel powers a Diesel Generator, while Fuel is what your rocket burns to reach space.
