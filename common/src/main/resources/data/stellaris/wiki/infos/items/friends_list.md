The Friends List keeps track of the players you trust.

[item=stellaris:friends_list]

Right-click on a player to add them to your friends list. 
If you are already friends with that player, right-clicking them again removes them from the list. 

The list is what makes a private [ref=stellaris:machines/antenna]Antenna[ref] shareable : right-click the antenna with the Friends List in hand and everyone on it becomes allowed to land there.
