The Blue Liquid is a strange fluid found in the lakes of the Moon.

[item=stellaris:blue_liquid_bucket]

[ref=stellaris:mobs/blue_fish]Blue Fish[ref] live in it as happily as in water, which is the first clue that it is not simply colored liquid. 

Scoop it up with a bucket : it is one of the four ingredients of the [ref=stellaris:items/vaccine]Vaccine[ref], so a trip to a lunar lake is part of curing the infection. 

Recent missions to the Moon report that all the astronauts experienced a strange sensation after going into it...
