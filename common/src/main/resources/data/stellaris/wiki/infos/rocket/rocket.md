The rocket is the whole point of Stellaris. It is what turns a base into a space program.

[item=stellaris:rocket]

**Building one** 

Assemble it in the Rocket Station tab of the [ref=stellaris:machines/engineering_station]Engineering Station[ref] : a Rocket Nose Cone on top, [ref=stellaris:items/titanium]Titanium[ref] ingots for the hull, four Rocket Fins and a Rocket Engine at the bottom. 



**Launching** 

Put down a [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket_launch_pad]Rocket Launch Pad[ref] and right-click its centre with the rocket to place it. Climb in, then feed it [ref=stellaris:items/diesel]Fuel[ref] through the fuel slot : a bucket or a [ref=stellaris:items/fluid_cell]Fluid Cell[ref] adds 1 000 mB at a time, up to a tank of 3 000 mB. 

Start the engine and a countdown of 10 seconds begins before the rocket lifts off. Each launch burns 1 000 mB, so a full tank is worth three trips. 
Make sure the sky above the pad is clear, the rocket refuses to start if anything blocks its path. 

Once you pass a height of 400 blocks the destination screen opens and you pick where to go. 
Landing is done by a [ref=stellaris:rocket/lander]Lander[ref], which brings you and your whole cargo down to the surface. 



**Modules** 

The rocket accepts modules, installed in the Upgrade tab of the Engineering Station. See [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket_modules]Rocket Modules[ref]. 



Stats : 
- Tank Capacity : 3 000 mB 
- Fuel per Launch : 1 000 mB 
- Countdown : 10 seconds 
- Climb Speed : 0.8 blocks per tick
