The fluid cell carries three times more fluid than a bucket, and it never leaves an empty container behind.

[item=stellaris:fluid_cell]

Place it in a machine's fluid slot to fill it or empty it, exactly like a bucket. It works with every fluid of the mod : oil, fuel, diesel, water, oxygen, hydrogen and blue liquid. 

Hold it and right-click while wearing a [ref=stellaris:items/space_suit]Space Suit[ref] and it pours its content straight into the suit's chestplate tank, so you can refuel your jet in the field. 



Stats : 
- Capacity : 3 000 mB 

It is one of The Stellar Company's greatest successes. Who needs buckets these days?
