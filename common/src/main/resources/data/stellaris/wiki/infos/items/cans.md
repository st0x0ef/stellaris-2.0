Cans are used to store food. You fill them with the [ref=stellaris:machines/vacuumator]Vacuumator[ref].

[item=stellaris:big_lime_can]

Cans are the only way to eat in space. Outside a room with oxygen, no ordinary food can be swallowed at all - only a can. 



**How to eat a can** 

Hold the can in one hand and a **potion in your other hand**, for example a simple water bottle, then right-click the can. Without something to drink you cannot get the food down. 
While you hold a can, the potion itself cannot be drunk, so nothing is wasted. 



**Sizes and colors** 

Cans come in the sixteen dye colors, and any of them can be recolored later. 
- A small can holds 10 nutrition. 
- A big can holds 20 nutrition. 

A can is not a single meal : keep feeding food into the same can in the Vacuumator until it reaches its limit.
