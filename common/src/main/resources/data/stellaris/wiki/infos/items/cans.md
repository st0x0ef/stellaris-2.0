Cans are used to store food. You fill them with the [ref=stellaris:machines/vacuumator]Vacuumator[ref].

[item=stellaris:big_lime_can]

Cans are the only way to eat in space. On a planet without a breathable atmosphere, no ordinary food can be swallowed at all. 



**How to eat a can** 

Hold the can and right-click it. You need something to drink to get the food down : 
- A [ref=stellaris:items/fluid_cell]Fluid Cell[ref] filled with **water**, anywhere in your inventory. Each can uses 100 mB, so a full cell lasts 30 meals. 
- Or a **potion** held in your other hand, for example a simple water bottle. It is used up. While you hold a can, the potion itself cannot be drunk, so nothing is wasted. 



**Sizes and colors** 

Cans come in the sixteen dye colors, and any of them can be recolored later. 
- A small can holds 10 nutrition. 
- A big can holds 20 nutrition. 

A can is not a single meal : keep feeding food into the same can in the Vacuumator until it reaches its limit.
