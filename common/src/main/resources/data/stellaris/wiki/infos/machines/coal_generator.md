The Coal Generator is a basic power generator that burns coal. It provides a steady supply of energy for your machines and devices, making it an essential first step for any off-earth colony.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/coal_generator width=96 height=96]



**How to use:** 
Place the coal generator and connect it to your power grid with [ref=stellaris:machines/cables]Cables[ref]. Add fuel to the fuel slot to start generating power. 
It also pushes energy directly into machines placed right next to it. 



Accepted fuels : Coal, Charcoal and Blocks of Coal. 



**Stats :** 
- Power Output : 45 FE/t 
- Internal Buffer : 720 000 FE 
- 1 Coal or Charcoal burns for about 533 ticks, so roughly 24 000 FE 
- 1 Block of Coal burns ten times longer, so roughly 240 000 FE
