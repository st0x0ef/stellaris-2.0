The launchpad allow you to launch rockets into space.

[item=stellaris:rocket_launch_pad]

To place a rocket, right-click with it on the launchpad.

You can craft the launchpad with eight block of [ref=stellaris:items/titanium]Titanium Plating Block[ref].

You can upgrade your launch pad with [ref=stellaris:rocket/launch_pad_towers]Launch Pad Towers[ref] by right clicking on it.
