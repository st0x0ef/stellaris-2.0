The launch pad is where a rocket stands before it leaves the ground.

[item=stellaris:rocket_launch_pad]

Craft it with eight [ref=stellaris:items/titanium]Titanium Plating Blocks[ref] in a ring. Placing the item builds the whole 3 x 3 platform at once, and breaking the centre takes the whole pad apart again. 

To place a rocket, right-click the centre of the pad with the [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket]Rocket[ref] in hand. 

The pad accepts two upgrades, both installed by right-clicking the centre of the pad with the item in hand : 
- [ref=stellaris:rocket/launch_pad_towers]Launch Pad Towers[ref]. 
- An [ref=stellaris:machines/antenna]Antenna[ref], which registers the pad as a landing waypoint. 

A [ref=stellaris:machines/cargo_unloader]Cargo Unloader[ref] placed next to the pad will empty the lander for you when a flight comes home.
