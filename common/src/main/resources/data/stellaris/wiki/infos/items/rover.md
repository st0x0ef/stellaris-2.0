The Rover is a drivable vehicle built for exploring the surface of other planets.

[item=stellaris:rover]

Craft it at a crafting table from iron ingots, a cable, an empty bucket, [ref=stellaris:items/titanium]Titanium[ref] ingots and redstone dust. 

Place it down, hop in and drive across hostile terrain in comfort, with room for a second passenger. It runs on [ref=stellaris:items/diesel]Diesel[ref] : drop a diesel bucket or a [ref=stellaris:items/fluid_cell]Fluid Cell[ref] in its fuel slot to refill the tank. 
Its tank is generous, so one bucket takes you a very long way. Drive carefully though, a rover at full speed hurts anything it runs into. 

To pick it back up, hit the empty rover while sneaking. It keeps its fuel and modules, but everything in its inventory drops on the ground. 



**Modules** 

Install modules at the [ref=stellaris:machines/engineering_station]Engineering Station[ref], in the Upgrade Station tab. The rover shares two of the [ref=stellaris:rocket/rocket_modules]Rocket Modules[ref] and has two of its own, all found in the chests of Moon Spheres. 
- **Cargo Module** : adds 2 more rows of inventory. 
- **Hydrogen Motor** : switches the engine over to hydrogen instead of diesel. 
- **Rover Tank Module** : doubles the fuel tank, from 3 000 to 6 000 mB. 
- **Rover Speed Module** : multiplies the top speed by 1.5. 

Note that the tank only holds one kind of fuel at a time. **Empty it** before switching motors.



Stats : 
- Base Tank : 3 000 mB of Diesel
- Consumption : 1 mB every 25 blocks driven 
- Base Inventory : 1 row, up to 3 rows with the cargo module
- Seats : 2
