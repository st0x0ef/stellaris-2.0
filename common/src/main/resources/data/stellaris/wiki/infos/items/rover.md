The Rover is a drivable vehicle built for exploring the surface of other planets.

[item=stellaris:rover]

Place it down, hop in and drive across hostile terrain in comfort. It runs on [ref=stellaris:items/diesel]Diesel[ref] : drop a diesel bucket or a [ref=stellaris:items/fluid_cell]Fluid Cell[ref] in its fuel slot and it refills 1 000 mB at a time. 
Its tank is generous, so one bucket takes you a very long way. Drive carefully though, a rover at full speed hurts anything it runs into. 



**Modules** 

Install modules at the [ref=stellaris:machines/engineering_station]Engineering Station[ref], in the Upgrade tab. 
- **Cargo module** : adds 2 more rows of inventory. 
- **Tank module** : doubles the fuel tank, from 3 000 to 6 000 mB. 
- **Speed module** : multiplies the top speed by 1.5. 
- **Hydrogen Motor** : switches the engine over to hydrogen instead of diesel. 

Note that the tank only accepts one kind of fuel at a time. Empty it before switching motors. 



Stats : 
- Base Tank : 3 000 mB of Diesel 
- Refuel : 1 000 mB per bucket or fluid cell 
- Consumption : 2 mB every 20 blocks driven 
- Base Inventory : 1 row, up to 3 rows with the cargo module
