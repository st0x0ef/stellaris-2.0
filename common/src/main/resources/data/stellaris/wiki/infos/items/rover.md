The Rover is a drivable vehicle built for exploring the surface of other planets.

[item=stellaris:rover]

Place it down, hop in and drive across hostile terrain in comfort. 



**Modules**

The rover can be customized with modules : 
- Cargo module adds inventory slots. 
- Tank module stores fluid for the trip. 
- Speed module makes the rover faster. 
- Hydrogen Motor powers the rover with hydrogen fuel.
