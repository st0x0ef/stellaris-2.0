The deep lab was meant to be a refuge. When the surface fell, the survivors dug down and sealed the tunnels behind them - and never understood that the [ref=stellaris:mobs/infection]Infection[ref] had come down with them, in someone's blood.

[entity=stellaris:heart_of_luna scale=18 width=150 height=100]

Something at the bottom of that complex has been feeding ever since.

The smaller parasites answer to it. They do not hunt for themselves; they bring it warmth. It has grown swollen with every colonist it took, and it does not move much, because it does not need to. Everything in those tunnels is already part of it.

Doctor Vey carried the last of her vaccine down to the core herself. If the infection was thickest anywhere, she reasoned, that was where the cure had to reach. She did not come back up. Her final log ends with four words : *end it at the source*.



**What we know** 

It does not breathe, and it cannot catch the thing it spreads. Its presence is felt from 48 blocks away, long before you see it.

It will not simply be killed. Twice in the fight it refuses to fall any further until it has answered - and until it does, every blow you land is simply refused. Whatever you walk in carrying, the descent takes as long as it takes.

It watches what is in front of it. Strike at its face, or send an arrow on a line it can read, and it turns the blow aside and stays guarded for several seconds. It has no such answer for what comes from behind.

And when it roars, the tunnels answer. For a quarter of a minute it hits twice as hard, and it leaves something in the air where it stood - a cloud that settles for a few seconds and sinks the infection into anything still standing in it. Do not be standing in it.

Its ray is the thing that kills. A shield turned into the beam takes barely a quarter off it. In the last stage the ray comes sooner.
