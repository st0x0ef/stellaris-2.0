The Vaccine is your cure against the parasite infection that spreads on the Moon.

[item=stellaris:vaccine]

The [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite]Lunar Parasite[ref] can attach to you and inflict the infection. To fight back, you must research a cure. 



First, capture a sample in a Pathogen Storage Cell :

[item=stellaris:pathogen_storage_cell]

Then use the [ref=stellaris:machines/laboratory]Laboratory[ref] to research the pathogen and produce a Vaccine. Applying it removes the infection and protects you.
