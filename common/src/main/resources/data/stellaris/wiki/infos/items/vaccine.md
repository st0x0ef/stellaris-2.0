The Vaccine is your cure against the parasite infection that spreads on the Moon.

[item=stellaris:vaccine]

The [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite]Lunar Parasite[ref] latches onto you and inflicts the infection, which damages you at random for as long as it lasts. [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite_villager]Parasite Villagers[ref] and their evolved form spread it too. To fight back, you must research a cure. 



**Step 1 - Collect** 

Kill Lunar Parasites yourself to get Parasites, and store them in a Pathogen Storage Cell (right-click with the cell in hand). 

[item=stellaris:pathogen_storage_cell]



**Step 2 - Research** 

Feed the filled cell to the [ref=stellaris:machines/laboratory]Laboratory[ref] and run the five research stages. 



**Step 3 - Craft** 

In the Laboratory's Vaccine tab, combine : 
- A Honey Bottle 
- A [ref=stellaris:items/blue_liquid]Blue Liquid[ref] Bucket 
- An Astrum Vitreus Cluster 
- A Parasite 



**Step 4 - Use** 

Right-click with the Vaccine in hand. It clears the infection you already have and makes you **permanently immune**, so parasites can never infect you again. 
Using it before the research is finished only tells you that you are not ready.
