The Oil Finder is used to find [ref=stellaris:items/oil]Oil[ref] in the world. It is a crucial item for space exploration, as oil is required to create diesel fuel, which powers the rocket.

[item=stellaris:oil_finder]

Right-click on a chunk to see if it contains oil. Then you can extract it with a [ref=stellaris:machines/pumpjack]Pumpjack[ref].
