The Oil Finder tells you whether the chunk you are standing in hides [ref=stellaris:items/oil]Oil[ref]. It is a crucial item for space exploration, since oil is what becomes the fuel that powers the rocket.

[item=stellaris:oil_finder]

Right-click anywhere and the exact amount of oil under your feet appears above your hotbar, in mB. Green means a rich deposit, orange a small one, red means nothing here. Then extract it with a [ref=stellaris:machines/pumpjack]Pumpjack[ref]. 

The Oil Finder runs on energy : it holds 1 000 FE and spends 1 FE per scan. Recharge it by leaving it in the item slot of a [ref=stellaris:machines/solar_panel]Solar Panel[ref] or another generator. 
Each scan also costs 2 points of durability, so it will eventually wear out. 

Once you have a [ref=stellaris:items/space_suit]Space Suit[ref], an Oil Finder module on the helmet does the same job continuously and over several chunks at once, without ever wearing out.
