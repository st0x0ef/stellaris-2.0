This is the **most** important module in all the galaxy ! Oxygen is the only thing that keeps you alive in space, and this module is the only way to breath it.t.

[item=stellaris:space_suit_oxygen_module_tier_1]

Every second, a normal player breathes **1 mB** of oxygen.

Having the oxygen module has a lot of advantages like being able to breathe in vacuum, underwater, and in any other place where there is no oxygen.
But be careful, sprinting and swimming burns oxygen twice as fast, and flying on the jets burns it four times as fast, so a long flight is paid for in air as well as fuel.



*In space, the most valuable resource is not money. It is oxygen.*

__TATHAN, Philosopher and Astronaut, 2026__



**How to store oxygen**
The module stores oxygen in your **helmet**, and you can refill it at an [ref=stellaris:machines/oxygen]Oxygen Distributor[ref] or from a [ref=stellaris:items/fluid_cell]Fluid Cell[ref] of oxygen held in hand.


**Tiers**
The module has three tiers that allows to you to carry more oxygen in your helmet : 1 200, 3 600 or 6 000 mB.
