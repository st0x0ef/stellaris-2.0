The Space Suit is what keeps you alive beyond Earth. Without it, the vacuum, cold and lack of oxygen would kill you in seconds.

[item=stellaris:space_suit_chestplate]

A full set is made of four pieces : 
- Helmet 
- Chestplate 
- Leggings 
- Boots 

Wear all four to be protected in space. 



**Modules**

The suit can be upgraded with modules, each available in three tiers : 
- Oxygen modules store the air you breathe, paired with an [ref=stellaris:machines/oxygen]Oxygen Distributor[ref] to refill. 
- Oil Finder modules add the [ref=stellaris:items/oil_finder]Oil Finder[ref] ability to your suit. 
- Diesel and Hydrogen tank modules carry fuel with you. 
- Jet modules let you fly. 
- Damage Protection modules increase your armor (iron, diamond then netherite tier).
