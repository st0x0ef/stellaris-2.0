The Space Suit is what keeps you alive beyond Earth. Anywhere without breathable air, you take damage every second until you find some - or until you put this on.

[item=stellaris:space_suit_chestplate]

A full set is made of four pieces : 
- Helmet 
- Chestplate 
- Leggings 
- Boots 

You must wear **all four** for the suit to count. Three pieces protect you from nothing. 

The suit alone is not enough either : it is the oxygen stored in the helmet that you actually breathe. With a filled oxygen module you spend 1 mB per second in vacuum, and you can even breathe underwater. Refill the helmet at an [ref=stellaris:machines/oxygen]Oxygen Distributor[ref] or from a [ref=stellaris:items/fluid_cell]Fluid Cell[ref] held in hand. 



**Modules** 

Install modules at the [ref=stellaris:machines/engineering_station]Engineering Station[ref], in the Upgrade tab. Each module only fits one piece of the suit. 

**Helmet** 
- Oxygen module, three tiers : 1 200, 3 600 or 14 400 mB of air. 
- Oil Finder module, three tiers : scans 5 x 5, 3 x 3 or 1 x 1 chunks around you and shows the total [ref=stellaris:items/oil]Oil[ref] on screen. A wider scan drains the helmet's energy faster, a narrower one tells you precisely which chunk to drill. 
- Night Vision module : see in the dark. 

**Chestplate** 
- Diesel tank module, three tiers : 1 000, 2 000 or 3 000 mB. 
- Hydrogen tank module, three tiers : 1 000, 2 000 or 3 000 mB. 
These carry the fuel your boots burn. 

**Boots** 
- Jet module, three tiers : lets you fly. Tier 1 burns 10 mB of fuel per tick, tier 2 burns 6 and tier 3 only 2, so the higher the tier the longer you stay airborne. 

**Any piece** 
- Damage Protection module, three tiers : gives that piece the armor of iron, diamond or netherite.
