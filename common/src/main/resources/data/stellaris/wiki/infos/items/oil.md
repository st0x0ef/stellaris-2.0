Oil is the base of everything that flies. Without it there is no fuel, and without fuel there is no space travel.

[item=stellaris:oil_bucket]

Oil is not a block you dig up : it is a hidden reserve stored in the chunk itself. Find a chunk that holds some with the [ref=stellaris:items/oil_finder]Oil Finder[ref], then extract it with a [ref=stellaris:machines/pumpjack]Pumpjack[ref] placed anywhere in that chunk. 

Roughly one chunk in thirty-two hides a deposit, and a deposit holds anywhere between 10 and 50 000 mB. The reserve is **finite** : once a chunk is drained, the Pumpjack there stops for good and you have to prospect elsewhere. 

You can also stumble on oil lakes on the surface or underground while exploring the Overworld. 

Send the oil to a [ref=stellaris:machines/fuel_refinery]Fuel Refinery[ref] to turn it into [ref=stellaris:items/diesel]Fuel and Diesel[ref].
