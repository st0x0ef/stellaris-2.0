The Galactic Rays Collector is a powerful machine that use the radioactive power of the sun to produce energy.
Attention! It also produces radioactive waste!

Every planet has a certain amount of solar radiation.

**Planets**:
- [ref=stellaris:planets/earth]Earth[ref] : 0 mCv
- [ref=stellaris:planets/moon]The Moon[ref] : 10 mCv
- [ref=stellaris:planets/mars]Mars[ref] : 50 mCv



**How to use : **
Place a Galactic Rays Collector under the sky in a planet with solar radiation. Add Radioactive Containers in the first slot.
Then, the machine will start generating energy and will also fill the radioactive containers with radioactive waste.


**Stats : **
- Power Output : (Planet Radiation) FE/t
