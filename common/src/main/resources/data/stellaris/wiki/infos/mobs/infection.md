It did not begin on the Moon. It arrived.

[item=stellaris:parasite]

A meteor came down harder than the models predicted, and the rock stayed warm for days afterwards. When the survey team cracked it open they found threads of something organic laced through the mineral - not fossils, because they moved when the lamp warmed them. Doctor Vey called them dormant spores. She was right, and by the time anyone acted on it the rock had already been carried inside.

In the colony it looked like nothing at all. A strange fever. Pale skin, aching joints, a far-off look. The clinic called it cabin sickness.

It was never cabin sickness. The fevered ones changed - skin cracking, eyes going dark, moving in the same slow way as the thing found in the rock. One colonist's last diary page describes barricading the door against a neighbour who had been a farmer the day before.

The [ref=stellaris:mobs/alien]Aliens[ref] at the sphere already knew what it was. They have watched it hollow out other worlds.



**Carrying it** 

A [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite]Lunar Parasite[ref] latches on and puts it into you. So do the [ref=stellaris:mobs/parasite_villager]Parasite Villagers[ref], which is what is left of the colonists. So does the cloud the [ref=stellaris:mobs/heart_of_luna]thing in the deep lab[ref] leaves behind when it roars.

It does not drain you steadily. It takes you **at random** - a point of health every few seconds, on no schedule you can read, for as long as it lasts. That is what makes it dangerous. An infection you caught at full health is an inconvenience. The same infection at three hearts, halfway up a cliff, is not. Treat it as a reason to turn back, never as a timer to sit out.

Nothing native to the Moon can catch it. The parasites, the [ref=stellaris:mobs/luna_shadow]Shadows[ref], the [ref=stellaris:mobs/star_crawler]Crawlers[ref], the Aliens and both of the things that guard them are all immune. Only what came from Earth can be hollowed out.



**Ending it** 

Milk will not touch it. There is exactly one way out, and the Stellar Company had to invent it : study the parasite in a [ref=stellaris:machines/laboratory]Laboratory[ref] until you understand it well enough to build a [ref=stellaris:items/vaccine]Vaccine[ref] from its own structure.

The colony's researchers got there in the end. It was too late for them. It does not have to be too late for you.



*Field note recovered in the tunnels*: the parasites hunt by warmth and sound. Honey burns them. Cold slows them. Do not let them touch bare skin.
