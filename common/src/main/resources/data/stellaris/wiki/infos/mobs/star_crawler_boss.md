The sphere was here long before we were.

[entity=stellaris:star_crawler_boss scale=20 width=150 height=100]

It is older than the regolith it rests on - seamless metal that our drills cannot scratch and our scanners cannot read. The colonists took it for a monument. The surveyor who wrote that report came to wonder whether it was a warning, left for us, that we arrived too late to understand.

Something grew up guarding it. The [ref=stellaris:mobs/star_crawler]Star Crawlers[ref] that skitter across the [ref=stellaris:planets/moon]Moon[ref] are small things; this is what one becomes when nothing stops it. It does not roam. It circles the sphere, and it has been circling a long time.

The [ref=stellaris:mobs/alien]Aliens[ref] who trade at the sphere's edge were not surprised by the parasite when we showed them a sample. They recoiled, and paid us to keep it away from them. They have watched this blight hollow out other worlds. Whatever the sphere is, they knew about it first.



**What we know** 

Come within 48 blocks and it knows. It turns, and it roars, and it will not be hurried.

Where the thing in the dungeon is soft and swollen, this one is plated. Hits skid off it and it barely feels a shove, so a slow weapon swung hard serves far better than a fast one.

It reads the distance between you and picks its answer accordingly, and it learns more answers as the fight wears on. Close in and it smashes the ground. Give it room and it charges. And at some point it will leave the ground entirely and come down on top of you - that is the one that ends fights.

There is no safe range. There are only trades.
