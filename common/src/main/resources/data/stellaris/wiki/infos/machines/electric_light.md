The Electric Light is a lamp you can dim. Instead of the fixed glow of a torch or a glowstone block, it shines anywhere between off and full daylight, and it costs more power the brighter you set it.

**How to use**
Place the light, connect it to a power source, then open the GUI and drag the slider. Step 0 turns the lamp off, steps 1 to 7 give the half lit look and steps 8 to 15 the full one. The lamp keeps its setting when you close the GUI. 

It only shines while it has energy. Cut the power and it goes dark without losing its setting, so it lights back up at the same brightness as soon as the power comes back. Wiring it through a switchable line is the easiest way to turn a whole base on and off. 

**Stats**
- Power Consumption : 1 FE every 150 ticks at brightness 1, up to 1 FE every 10 ticks at brightness 15 
- Brightness Range : 0 to 15 
- Internal Buffer : 1 000 FE
