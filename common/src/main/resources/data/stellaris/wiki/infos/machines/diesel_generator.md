The Diesel Generator produces energy by burning diesel. It is the strongest generator of the mod.

[item=stellaris:diesel_generator scale=5]

Feed it [ref=stellaris:items/diesel]Diesel[ref] through a [ref=stellaris:machines/pipes]Pipe[ref], a bucket or a [ref=stellaris:items/fluid_cell]Fluid Cell[ref] in its input slot, and it will generate a steady supply of energy for your machines. 
Diesel is refined from [ref=stellaris:items/oil]Oil[ref] in the [ref=stellaris:machines/fuel_refinery]Fuel Refinery[ref]. 



Stats : 
- Fuel : Diesel only 
- Power Output : 5 FE/t 
- Fuel Consumption : 1 mB every 5 ticks, so 25 FE per mB 
- Tank Capacity : 10 000 mB 
- Internal Buffer : 60 000 FE
