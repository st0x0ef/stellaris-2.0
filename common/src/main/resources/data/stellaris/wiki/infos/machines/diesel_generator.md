The Diesel Generator produces energy by burning diesel.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/diesel_generator width=96 height=96]

Feed it [ref=stellaris:items/diesel]Diesel[ref] and it will generate a steady supply of energy for your machines. 
Diesel is refined from oil in the [ref=stellaris:machines/fuel_refinery]Fuel Refinery[ref]. 



Stats : 
- Fuel : Diesel
