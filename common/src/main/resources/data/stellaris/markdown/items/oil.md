Oil is the base for traveling in space.

[item=stellaris:oil_bucket]

You can find oil in a chunk with the [ref=stellaris:items/oil_finder]Oil Finder[ref]. 
You can then use it to create diesel fuel, which is required to power the rocket and explore space.

When you find oil in a chunk, you can extract it with a [ref=stellaris:machines/pumpjack]Pumpjack[ref].
