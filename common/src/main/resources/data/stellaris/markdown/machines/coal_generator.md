The Coal Generator is a basic power generator that uses coal as fuel. It provides a steady supply of energy for your machines and devices, making it an essential component for any off-earth colony.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/coal_generator width=96 height=96]



How to use: 
Place the coal generator in a suitable location and connect it to your power grid. Add coal to the fuel slot to start generating power. The generator will consume coal over time to produce energy. 

Stats: 
- Power Output: 3 FE/t 
- Fuel Type: Coal 
- Fuel Consumption: 1 coal produces 4800 FE
