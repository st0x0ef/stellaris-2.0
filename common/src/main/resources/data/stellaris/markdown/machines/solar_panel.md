The Solar Panel is a basic power generator that uses sun light as fuel. It provides a steady supply of energy for your machines and devices, making it an essential component for any off-earth colony.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/solar_panel width=96 height=96]



How to use : 
Place the solar panel in a suitable location with sun access and connect it to your power grid. The generator will produce energy over time. 

Stats : 
- Power Output : 1 FE/t
