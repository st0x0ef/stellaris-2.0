The Electrolyzer is a machine that can be used to produce oxygen and hydrogen from water. It is commonly used in space stations and other structures to provide a sustainable source of breathable air and fuel.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/electrolyzer width=96 height=96]



How to use: 
Place the electrolyzer. Connect the machine to a power source, then open the GUI to insert water into the second slot. The machine starts producing oxygen and hydrogen automatically.
