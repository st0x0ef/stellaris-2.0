The Cargo Unloader allows you to automatically collect items and the rocket item from inside a lander.

[image=stellaris:textures/wiki/rendered_blocks/cargo_unloader width=96 height=96]



How to use : 
Charge the cargo unloader with energy and place it next to a launchpad or where you expect a lander to be. When a lander is present, the cargo unloader will automatically collect the items and the rocket item inside the lander and transfer them to its inventory. 

Stats : 
- Power Consumption : 100 FE per item extracted.
